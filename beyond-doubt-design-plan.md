# Beyond Doubt design plan

Scope: index-beyond-doubt.html. Visitor mode: Experience, with direct access to the supplied shops and business contact information.

The user pins the original template's composition and motion. This is a constrained rebrand, not a replacement layout. Code-led implementation uses the supplied assets.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A monochrome jersey studio. Preserve the spacious, orbiting collection instead of replacing it with a conventional product grid.

OWN-WORLD: Chalk gray, carbon ink, the actual italic Beyond Doubt wordmark, thin rules, compact uppercase navigation, and the product graphics as the color source.

STORY: Recognize the brand, explore five actual jersey designs, inspect front and back, then shop at the supplied stores or find the Quezon City business details.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Original corner navigation and labels; a faint oversized brand backdrop behind a five-piece orbit; generous negative space; collection index and exploration controls along the bottom. The shop action stays in the upper-right corner. The centered jersey is the dominant object.

FORM: Candidate 5, monochrome photo studio, seed 8e2b5f25. User-pinned template overrides foreign topology. Alternatives considered: racing paddock, garment label, skate catalog, jersey locker, photo studio, local shop window, monochrome lookbook. The supplied original fixes layout. Arcade and oscilloscope forms lose product clarity; industrial quote grammar risks confusing another fashion identity; broadside and garden forms contradict the supplied template. Neutral product marketing contributes legibility without changing the orbit. Keep precise states, disciplined neutrals, and spatial separation from those comparisons.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Implementation

- Preserve original index.html byte-for-byte.
- Derive a transparent cropped wordmark and front/back product views from the supplied imagery. Preserve artwork; exclude the duplicate from the collection count.
- Retain orbit, drag inertia, entrance, detail zoom, and a reduced-motion mode. Add pointer parallax and usable previous/next controls.
- Replace fictional color options with front/back selection and simulated checkout with the real shop destinations.
- Use descriptive design names, without prices, sizes, fabrics, availability, or official model-name claims.
- Put the supplied address, telephone, profile history, supplied verification/rating, and social/store links in an accessible business drawer. Do not publish stale 'open now' or 'updated four hours ago' as live information.
- Validate desktop/mobile rendering, front/back changes, keyboard controls, drawer behavior, local assets, and preservation of the original.
